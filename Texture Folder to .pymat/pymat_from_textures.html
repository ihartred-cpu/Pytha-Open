pymat from Textures

Turn a folder of texture images into a PYTHA material library (.pymat), with colour, roughness/bump wiring and preview thumbnails. Everything runs in this page — nothing is uploaded, and your textures are never changed.

Unofficial community tool, not affiliated with PYTHA Lab. The .pymat layout was worked out from PYTHA V26 files; untested on other versions. Work on copies, keep backups, and close PYTHA before swapping a library in.
